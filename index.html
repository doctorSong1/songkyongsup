About the Teacher

Meet your guide to Korean fluency

My Teaching Philosophy

I emphasize building speaking confidence from day one. By balancing clear pronunciation, essential grammar, and practical vocabulary, my students learn how to structure sentences independently instead of just memorizing phrases.

Tailored Methodology

Every learner is unique. After assessing your strengths and interests (K-dramas, business, travel, or TOPIK preparation), I create customized lesson materials, write key notes during our sessions, and share them directly with you.
